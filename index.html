🎮 How to Play

Jump to avoid cars 🚗 and trash 🗑️. Collect leaves 🍃 and flowers 🌸 to gain points and plant trees. Each item collected = +10 points. The game gets faster as you score!

Controls: Press SPACE, UP ARROW, tap the Jump button, or click the canvas.

🌱 About This Game

Eco Sprint is a fun way to learn about environmental impact. Every obstacle you avoid represents pollution prevention, and every item you collect symbolizes environmental action. Challenge yourself to beat your high score while raising awareness about sustainability!

💾 Your high score is saved in your browser's local storage.
